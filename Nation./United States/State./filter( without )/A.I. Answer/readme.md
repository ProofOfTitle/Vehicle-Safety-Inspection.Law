As of 2026, Louisiana (which famously referred to its windshield safety stickers as "brake tags") has eliminated statewide mandatory vehicle safety inspection stickers for personal passenger vehicles, transitioning to a registration-tied QR code system. U.S. States With No Periodic Safety Inspections

If you are referring to U.S. states that do not require regular or periodic vehicle safety inspections for personal passenger cars, the following states have no routine statewide safety inspection mandate: [1] (https://worldpopulationreview.com/state-rankings/states-without-vehicle-inspection)
• Alaska
• Arkansas
• Florida
• Iowa
• Michigan
• Minnesota
• Mississippi
• Montana
• North Dakota
• South Carolina
• South Dakota
• Texas (eliminated non-commercial safety inspections)
• Washington
• Wyoming
• New Hampshire (eliminated passenger vehicle safety inspections)
(Note: While these states do not mandate routine safety inspection stickers, local law enforcement can still pull over and cite a driver if a vehicle has obvious mechanical defects like a broken light or bald tires). [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NwOBqUYVFzk&t=636)
